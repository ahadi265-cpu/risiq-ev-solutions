Live Demo

Verify a certificate now.

Type a certificate ID or simulate a QR scan. The record is fetched from the public registry, its issuer signature checked, and the full certificate rendered — including the cell-level degradation map behind the headline number.

To check a certificate is genuine, compare every field here against the paper or PDF copy in your hand — vehicle, VIN, state of health, grade, test date. If anything differs, treat the copy as invalid and contact RISIQ directly.
