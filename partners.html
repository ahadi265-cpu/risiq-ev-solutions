Insurers

Price the risk you're already carrying.

Today there's no way to price battery risk at underwriting or at claims. Policies become guesses — and mispriced risk and fraud slip straight through.

  • Price battery risk accurately at underwriting and at claims.
  • Launch new EV insurance and battery-warranty products.
  • Cut claims disputes and battery-related fraud.
Co-design an insurance product

Why this changes underwriting

A signed, physically-measured SoH figure — not a self-reported BMS number — gives your actuaries a real variable to underwrite against, on the exact locked, imported EVs your policyholders already drive.

Banks & MFIs

Lend against an asset you can finally value.

Five-year auto loans are written against a battery that can lose half its value in two. Unpriceable collateral makes financing the #1 barrier to EV adoption.

  • Value the asset at origination and at resale.
  • Size loans and residual values with confidence.
  • De-risk EV lending at scale, not deal by deal.
Co-design a certificate-backed loan

Why this changes lending

A grade and a measured usable capacity at loan origination — and again at resale — turns the battery from an unknown into collateral you can actually model residuals against.

Importers & Dealers

Sell faster, at a premium, with proof.

Grey-market imports, no OEM service centres, no proof of health. Buyers hesitate, disputes follow, and good used EVs sit unsold.

  • Intake QC — know exactly what you're bringing in.
  • Warranty analytics across your whole intake.
  • Sell faster, at a premium, with buyer trust built in.
Certify your yard

Why this changes sales

A QR-verified certificate, handed to the buyer at the point of sale, replaces "trust me" with a public record they can scan and check in under 2 seconds.

Government & Fleets

A national standard for battery health.

Consumer protection, road and fire safety, and safeguarding the hard currency the whole EV pivot was meant to save — all rest on knowing which batteries are actually safe.

  • Fleet safety and uptime across public and private fleets.
  • Real consumer protection for used-EV buyers.
  • A credible, independent national standard for battery health.
Discuss a recognised standard

Why this changes policy

Independent, manufacturer-agnostic certification gives regulators a trust standard that works on every locked import already on the road — no OEM cooperation required to enforce it.
